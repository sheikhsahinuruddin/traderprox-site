About the data

Calculated live: clock, sun position, day/night boundary, sunrise/sunset.
Reference imagery (not real-time): surface, night lights (satellite-derived, not current electricity use) and clouds.
Live feeds: earthquakes (USGS); weather & air quality (Open-Meteo) for a selected place; the Live map type shows yesterday’s NASA satellite photo. Selecting a place sends its approximate coordinates (≈1 km) to Open-Meteo, and tapping the map near the ground sends that point to Nominatim for its name.
Map & places: OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth.
