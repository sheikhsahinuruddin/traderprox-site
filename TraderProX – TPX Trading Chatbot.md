## Trading tools, visualised

Tap “Ask TPX AI” on any card to get a full explanation in the chat.

Price action

### Candlestick Charts

Each candle shows one time period. Green means price closed higher, red means lower. Wicks show the high and low.

Trend

### Moving Averages

The average price drawn as a smooth line. Price above the line suggests an uptrend, below it a downtrend.

Momentum

### RSI Indicator

A 0 to 100 meter. Above 70 can mean overbought (pullback risk), below 30 can mean oversold (bounce chance).

Levels

### Support & Resistance

Support is a zone where price tends to stop falling. Resistance is where it tends to stop rising.

Volatility

### Bollinger Bands

Two bands around price. Tight bands often come before a big move, wide bands mean high volatility.

Retracement

### Fibonacci Levels

Shows how far price may pull back after a big move. The 50% to 61.8% zone is watched most closely.
